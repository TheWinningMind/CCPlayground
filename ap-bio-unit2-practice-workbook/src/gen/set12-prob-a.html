Problem 1 | Equal SA:V but unequal total area

9 POINTS | Extended response

Evidence supplied in the problem:

Object Dimension
Sphere radius = 1 µm
Cube side = 2 µm

(a) Calculate the sphere’s SA:V. [2 points]

  • Point 1: Sphere SA = 4π(1²) = 4π µm².

  • Point 2: Sphere V = (4/3)π(1³) = 4π/3 µm³, so SA/V = 3 µm⁻¹.

(b) Calculate the cube’s SA:V. [2 points]

  • Point 1: Cube SA = 6(2²) = 24 µm².

  • Point 2: Cube V = 2³ = 8 µm³, so SA/V = 3 µm⁻¹.

(c) Evaluate the statement “the equal ratios prove equal total exchange under all conditions.” [3 points]

  • Point 1: The two models have equal SA:V but unequal total area (4π versus 24 µm²).

  • Point 2: Total exchange additionally depends on permeability and driving gradients.

  • Point 3: A ratio alone cannot establish an equal total flux.

(d) Explain why these models can nevertheless illustrate a common scaling principle. [2 points]

  • Point 1: Both show that area can be compared relative to contained volume.

  • Point 2: SA:V is informative about exchange capacity per unit volume.

Total for Problem 1: 9 points.

Problem 4 | Graphing a saturating biological response

Evidence supplied in the problem:

Intensity (a.u.) Mean response (a.u.) SEM (a.u.)
1 12 1
2 19 2
3 23 2
4 24 2

Model graph: means with SEM error bars and labeled axes.

(a) Construct a graph of the means on the supplied blank axes. Include appropriate labels and units. [2 points]

  • Point 1: Label x as treatment intensity and y as mean response, both in arbitrary units.

  • Point 2: Plot the four means: (1,12), (2,19), (3,23), and (4,24).

(b) Add SEM error bars to the graph and state their interval at treatment intensity 3. [2 points]

  • Point 1: Plot SEM error bars about all four mean values using the supplied SEM data.

  • Point 2: At intensity 3, the SEM interval is 23 ± 2, from 21 to 25 response units.

(c) Describe the observed relationship without claiming statistical significance. [3 points]

  • Point 1: Response rises from 12 to 23 units between intensities 1 and 3.

  • Point 2: The increase from intensity 3 to 4 is small (23 to 24).

  • Point 3: The trend is consistent with leveling off; no formal significance test is provided.

(d) Explain why SEM overlap should not be treated as a complete significance test. [2 points]

  • Point 1: SEM describes precision of the mean estimate, not a direct hypothesis-test result.

  • Point 2: An appropriate statistical test and design information are needed for a significance claim.

Total for Problem 4: 9 points.
