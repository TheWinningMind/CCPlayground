Detailed rationales

Q01 • Correct answer: C • Practice 5.A

The cube has area 6(1²) = 6 cm² and volume 1³ = 1 cm³, so SA:V = 6:1.

Q02 • Correct answer: B • Practice 2.D

Surface area scales with length squared, whereas volume scales with length cubed.

Q03 • Correct answer: D • Practice 2.D

Volume scales with the cube of a linear dimension; surface area scales with its square.

Q04 • Correct answer: A • Practice 5.A

A rectangular solid has two faces of each area lw, lh, and wh.

Q05 • Correct answer: C • Practice 5.A

For a sphere SA/V = 3/r; therefore the smaller sphere has twice the ratio.

Q06 • Correct answer: D • Practice 5.A

Area has units length² and volume has units length³, so their ratio has units 1/length.

Q07 • Correct answer: B • Practice 1.A

The exposed surface provides the interface through which thermal energy can be exchanged.

Q08 • Correct answer: A • Practice 1.A

Demand is related to cellular volume, while exchange capacity depends partly on available membrane area.

Q09 • Correct answer: C • Practice 2.D

Flattening an equal-volume shape generally increases its surface area, while a sphere minimizes area for a fixed volume.

Q10 • Correct answer: B • Practice 1.A

Microvilli increase the plasma membrane area available for exchange without requiring proportional increases in internal volume.

Q11 • Correct answer: D • Practice 1.A

Root hairs are extensions that increase the exchange surface at the soil interface.

Q12 • Correct answer: C • Practice 2.D

Smaller bodies have a larger exposed-area-to-volume ratio, allowing more proportional heat exchange under comparable conditions.

Q13 • Correct answer: B • Practice 5.A

SA = 4πr² and V = (4/3)πr³.

Q14 • Correct answer: A • Practice 5.A

SA=2(1+4+4)=18 cm²; V=4 cm³; SA/V=18/4=4.5 cm⁻¹.

Q15 • Correct answer: C • Practice 2.D

Extra membrane area increases surface area while volume is approximately constant.

Q16 • Correct answer: D • Practice 2.D

For cubes SA/V = 6/s, which falls as side length increases.

Q17 • Correct answer: B • Practice 5.A

The ratios are 6/2=3 μm⁻¹ and 6/4=1.5 μm⁻¹.

Q18 • Correct answer: C • Practice 6.B

Surface area alone does not establish transport rate; transport depends on permeability, gradients, and other variables.

Q19 • Correct answer: A • Practice 5.A

V=(4/3)π(2³)=(32/3)π μm³.

Q20 • Correct answer: D • Practice 1.A

The CED identifies a typical inverse relationship between size and metabolic rate per unit body mass. This is a broad trend, not an absolute rule.

Q21 • Correct answer: C • Practice 5.A

The 3-cm cube has much more total exposed area (54 cm² versus 6 cm²) yet a lower fractional penetration (34.9% versus 78.4%).

Q22 • Correct answer: B • Practice 5.A

Fraction penetrated = (8−4.096)/8 = 0.488, or 48.8%.

Q23 • Correct answer: D • Practice 5.A

The unpenetrated side is 4−2(0.20)=3.6 cm, so penetrated fraction = 1−3.6³/4³ = 0.271.

Q24 • Correct answer: A • Practice 6.B

The assumed penetration depth is the same in each cube, but a greater fraction of a small cube lies within that depth.

Q25 • Correct answer: C • Practice 6.C

The 3-cm cube has 27−17.576=9.424 cm³ penetrated, greater than the 1-cm cube’s 0.784 cm³, despite its smaller fraction.

Q26 • Correct answer: B • Practice 5.A

Equal volumes make per-volume exchange proportional to surface area; the ratio C:A = 40/24 ≈ 1.67.

Q27 • Correct answer: A • Practice 6.E

Total exchange under an equal per-area flux is proportional to area; model C has 40 μm².

Q28 • Correct answer: D • Practice 6.B

SA/V predictions depend on other conditions being equal; a lower permeability can outweigh larger exposed area.

Q29 • Correct answer: C • Practice 3.C

A vs C varies exposed area/shape while retaining equal volume and specified membrane conditions.

Q30 • Correct answer: B • Practice 5.A

32/8 = 4 μm⁻¹; it lies between models B and C.

Q31 • Correct answer: D • Practice 2.D

The radius of R is four times that of P, so its surface area is 4² = 16 times as great, even though its SA/V ratio is one fourth.

Q32 • Correct answer: C • Practice 5.A

Radius increases fourfold, so area increases 4²=16× and volume 4³=64×.

Q33 • Correct answer: D • Practice 6.E

A larger similar sphere has a smaller SA/V ratio and lower potential heat exchange per mass under equivalent surface conditions.

Q34 • Correct answer: B • Practice 6.B

External coverings and thermoregulation can change exchange rates beyond what geometry alone predicts.

Q35 • Correct answer: A • Practice 5.A

R has half the SA/V of Q (0.75/1.5); predicted heat loss per gram is 1.5×0.5=0.75.

Q36 • Correct answer: C • Practice 4.B

B has greater membrane area and uptake than A; both lack the inhibitor, making this comparison the most relevant.

Q37 • Correct answer: D • Practice 5.A

The prediction is 12×(840/400)=25.2 pmol/cell/min, which is 1.2 above the observed 24.

Q38 • Correct answer: B • Practice 6.B

B and C have the same measured area, yet uptake is lower under transporter inhibition.

Q39 • Correct answer: A • Practice 5.A

12 pmol/cell/min divided by 400 μm²/cell = 0.030 pmol μm⁻² min⁻¹.

Q40 • Correct answer: C • Practice 3.D

An intervention on root-hair formation with a matched control and transporter controls better tests causality than correlation alone.
