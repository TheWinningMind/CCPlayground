01. Ribosome — Ribosomes occur in all known cellular life and perform translation.

02. Rough endoplasmic reticulum (RER) — Membrane-bound ribosomes synthesize proteins directed into the secretory pathway.

03. Smooth endoplasmic reticulum (SER) — The SER is an endomembrane compartment primarily associated here with lipid production.

04. Golgi apparatus — Golgi cisternae process and sort cellular products.

05. Lysosome — Lysosomes break down macromolecules and worn-out cellular components.

06. Vacuole — Vacuoles have storage and osmoregulatory roles that vary among cells.

07. Central vacuole — Water accumulation in a central vacuole supports turgor.

08. Crista — Cristae are infoldings of the inner mitochondrial membrane.

09. Mitochondrial matrix — The matrix is bounded by the inner mitochondrial membrane.

10. Mitochondrion — Mitochondria contain specialized membranes involved in cellular energy conversion.

11. Stroma — The stroma surrounds the thylakoid system of the chloroplast.

12. Thylakoid — Thylakoid membranes compartmentalize chloroplast activities.

13. Granum — A granum is a stack of thylakoids; the plural is grana.

14. Nuclear envelope — The nuclear envelope forms a selectively regulated boundary around the nucleus.

15. Transport vesicle — Vesicles deliver and move cargo among endomembrane compartments.

16. Endomembrane system — These components cooperate to synthesize, modify, package, and move cell products.

17. Golgi apparatus — Cargo commonly moves from ER to Golgi before delivery.

18. Plasma membrane — The plasma membrane is a selectively permeable biological boundary.

19. Nucleolus — The nucleolus is a non-membranous nuclear subregion associated with ribosome assembly.

20. Chloroplast — Chloroplasts contain thylakoids and stroma for photosynthetic functions.

21. Nucleus — The nucleus is enclosed by a nuclear envelope.

22. Rough endoplasmic reticulum — Ribosomes stud the RER surface.

23. Golgi apparatus — Golgi cisternae process and sort cell products.

24. Mitochondrion — Mitochondria possess cristae within a double membrane.

25. Lysosome — Lysosomes are membrane-bound digestive compartments.
