EXTENDED PROBLEM • 9 POINTS | Skill emphasis: 2.C, 3.C, 4.B, 6.E

Researchers label a plasma-membrane protein, bleach a small region, and measure fluorescence recovery in two membrane preparations. Percentages represent recovered fluorescence relative to prebleach intensity.

Problem 4 | The role of cholesterol in membrane organization

EXTENDED PROBLEM • 9 POINTS | Skill emphasis: 4.B, 6.B

Two artificial animal-cell membrane preparations have identical phospholipid mixtures and proteins, but one includes a moderate amount of cholesterol. Researchers measure membrane order at a low and a high temperature.

Preparation Order at 10°C Order at 40°C
No cholesterol 0.82 0.31
With cholesterol 0.65 0.5

(a) Interpret the change in the order index of the preparation without cholesterol. [2 points]

(b) Describe how the cholesterol-containing preparation differs. [2 points]

(c) Explain how the data support a temperature-buffering role for cholesterol. [3 points]

(d) Identify one limitation in applying these artificial-membrane results to intact cells. [2 points]
