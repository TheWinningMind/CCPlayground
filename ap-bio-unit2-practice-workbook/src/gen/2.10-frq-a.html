FRQ 2 · Evaluating the Endosymbiotic Hypothesis (9 points)
Criterion One-point scoring standard
a1 States that an ancestral host cell incorporated a free-living bacterium.
a2 States that the retained endosymbiont became integrated as the mitochondrion over evolutionary time.
b1 Identifies mitochondria with the sampled alphaproteobacterial lineage.
b2 Identifies chloroplasts with the sampled cyanobacterial lineage.
c Uses correct numerical comparison (86% vs 58% for mitochondrion OR 91% vs 54% for chloroplast).
d1 Explains that shared homologous sequences reflect inherited genetic information.
d2 Explains that similarity in size alone may occur due to functional or physical constraints and does not establish ancestry.
e1 Proposes valid independent evidence, such as a second conserved gene phylogeny or organellar ribosomal affinity.
e2 Explains that concordance with bacterial relatives strengthens the hypothesis beyond one sequence result.

Important boundary: the provided percentages are hypothetical classroom examples, not empirical phylogenetic sequence-identity estimates. Evidence from more than one gene and different methods would strengthen inference.
