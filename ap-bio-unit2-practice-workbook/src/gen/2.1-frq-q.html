At four times after the pulse, the investigators measure the percentage of the recovered labeled protein in each compartment. Each row sums to 100%. The values in the final row come from a separate sample treated with Drug X, which is suspected to impair protein export from the Golgi complex.

(a) (i) Describe the function of ribosomes in synthesizing the labeled proteins. (ii) Explain why ribosomes associated with rough ER are involved in producing proteins destined for secretion. [2 points]

(b) (i) Identify the time at which the highest percentage of labeled protein is detected in the Golgi complex in untreated cells. (ii) Calculate the increase, in percentage points, in labeled protein detected outside untreated cells between 10 and 30 minutes. [2 points]

(c) (i) Describe the changes between 0 and 30 minutes in the proportions of labeled protein in the rough ER and outside the cell. (ii) Explain how the time-course results support the involvement of the Golgi complex in the secretory pathway. [2 points]

(iii) Describe one additional measurement that would help exclude reduced initial protein synthesis as an alternative explanation. [3 points]
