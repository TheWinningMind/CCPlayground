FRQ 1 · Structure–Function Analysis of a Transmembrane Protein [9 points]
Part Points Credit-worthy response / required reasoning
a 1 Hydrophilic phosphate-containing heads face the extracellular and cytosolic aqueous environments.
a 1 Hydrophobic fatty acid tails face one another in the bilayer interior.
b 1 Nonpolar region-B surface residues are compatible with the hydrophobic fatty acid tail core.
b 1 A/C have polar or charged surface residues that can contact aqueous solutions outside and inside the cell.
c 1 88 − 31 = 57 percentage points.
c 1 (88 − 31)/88 × 100 = 64.8% relative decrease (accept 65%).
d 1 Membrane fraction is much smaller for variant than wild type despite similar total protein amounts.

e 1 Membrane association would likely increase toward wild-type levels because compatible hydrophobic contacts are restored.

Scoring emphasis: Correctly distinguish 57 percentage points from 64.8% relative decrease. Credit any reasonable limitation concerning folding, trafficking, or the need for localization controls.

FRQ 2 · Testing the Fluid Mosaic Model with Photobleaching [9 points]
Part Points Credit-worthy response / required reasoning
a 1 The fluorescence intensity in the defined bleached membrane region is measured over time relative to its prebleach value.
a 1 Signal recovery is consistent with unbleached fluorescent molecules moving laterally into the measured region.
b 1 A: (55−15)/20 = 2.00 percentage points/s.
b 1 B: (21−14)/20 = 0.35 percentage points/s.
c 1 Protein A is identified as showing greater 60-second recovery.
c 1 A reaches 74% compared with B at 29% (or the 45-point difference).
d 1 Valid manipulated comparison: restrict lateral diffusion in one group and compare with matched sham-treated cells, keeping labeling and bleaching comparable.
d 1 Predict reduced/slower fluorescence recovery when lateral movement is restricted.
e 1 B shows some recovery, and the observation window is limited to 60 s, so permanent immobility is not supported.

Scoring emphasis: FRAP recovery is indirect evidence for lateral mobility; fluorescence recovery should not be described as proof of newly synthesized molecules entering the region under the stated short observation window.

FRQ 4 · Cholesterol and Membrane Organization [4 points]
Part Points Credit-worthy response / required reasoning
a 1 Membrane cholesterol content (control versus cholesterol-reduced treatment).

The result is condition-specific. Do not reward categorical statements that cholesterol always lowers fluidity or protein mobility.
