Part 3 · Extra Practice

Who Am I? Vocabulary Challenge

Precise vocabulary earns points on the AP Biology Exam. Each clue below describes a term, molecule, property, bond, interaction, or structure from Unit 1. Write the term that best fits each clue. Use the figures when a clue refers to them. Answers are in Part 4.

How to Use This Section

Work one section at a time. Mark any clue you had to guess, then reread that part of the lessons before checking the answers. Return to the marked clues a few days later.
