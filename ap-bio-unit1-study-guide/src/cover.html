Master the chemistry that the rest of AP® Biology is built on.

Unit 1 introduces the ideas you will use all year: how water’s polarity makes life possible, how cells build and break down macromolecules, and how a molecule’s structure determines its function. This guide explains each idea clearly, then gives you the practice you need to answer exam-style questions with confidence.

  • Focused lessons on all six Unit 1 topics: water, elements of life, macromolecules, protein structure, and nucleic acids
  • Annotated diagrams and four one-page mind maps
  • Misconception alerts and exam tips on every topic
  • 58 multiple-choice questions with full explanations, plus 175 “Who Am I?” vocabulary questions
  • Three free-response questions with point-by-point scoring guidelines
  • Master comparison tables, a one-page review sheet, glossary, and score tracker

Ideal for students enrolled in AP Biology, for summer preparation, and for review before unit tests and the AP Exam.
