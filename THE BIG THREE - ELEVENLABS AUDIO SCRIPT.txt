Most people are not destroyed by what happens to them...

They are destroyed by... the meaning they give to it...

One rejection becomes... "I'm worthless."...

One failure becomes... "My life is over."...

The event lasts minutes... The interpretation... becomes a lifelong prison...

The Big Three...

First... PERCEPTION...

Separate facts... from the stories your mind invents...

Rejection is a fact... "I'm worthless" is a story...

Never allow a temporary event... to become your permanent identity...

Second... ACTION...

Stop negotiating with your excuses... Make the call... Face the fear... Start again...

Clarity without action... is intelligent procrastination...

The world does not reward what you intend... It responds to what you do...

Third... WILL...

Accept what you cannot control... The past... People's opinions... Closed doors...

Acceptance is not surrender... It is refusing to waste your strength... fighting reality...

The Stoic, Cleanthes, said... "Fate guides the willing... but drags the unwilling."...

When life strikes... ask three questions...

PERCEPTION... Am I seeing reality... or inventing suffering?...

ACTION... What can I do right now?...

WILL... What must I finally let go?...

You cannot command every storm... But you can command your judgment... your actions... and your response...

Master these three... and life may still shake your world... But it will no longer own your mind...
