See the cell from the inside out—and master AP® Biology Unit 2.

Unit 2 is where biology becomes cellular. You will learn what every organelle does, why cells stay small, how the fluid mosaic membrane controls what enters and leaves, how water moves by osmosis, and how compartments and endosymbiosis shaped the eukaryotic cell. This guide explains each idea step by step, then gives you the practice you need to answer exam-style questions with confidence.

  • Clear lessons on all eleven Unit 2 topics, from organelles to the origins of compartmentalization
  • Labeled diagrams of animal and plant cells, the membrane, and every transport mechanism
  • Step-by-step math: surface area-to-volume ratios and water potential
  • Misconception alerts, exam tips, and three one-page mind maps
  • 83 multiple-choice questions with full explanations, plus 100 “Who Am I?” vocabulary questions
  • Three free-response questions with point-by-point scoring guidelines
  • Master comparison tables, a one-page review sheet, glossary, and score tracker

Ideal for students enrolled in AP Biology, for summer preparation, and for review before unit tests and the AP Exam.
