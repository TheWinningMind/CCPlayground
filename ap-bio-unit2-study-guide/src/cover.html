See inside the cell, and master the unit that the rest of AP® Biology is built on.

Unit 2 takes you from molecules to living cells: the organelles that build, ship, and power, the membranes that decide what gets in and out, and the surprising origins of mitochondria and chloroplasts. This guide explains every idea clearly and then gives you more practice than ever, including the calculations and lab analysis the AP Exam loves to test.

  • Focused lessons on all eleven Unit 2 topics, from organelles and cell size to membrane transport, water potential, and endosymbiosis
  • Annotated diagrams, five one-page mind maps, and label-the-cell diagram practice
  • The Unit 2 Math Toolkit: surface area-to-volume and water potential, step by step
  • Lab Focus: diffusion and osmosis experiments to graph and analyze
  • 101 multiple-choice questions with full explanations, plus 200 “Who Am I?” vocabulary questions
  • Four free-response questions with point-by-point scoring guidelines
  • Master comparison tables, a one-page review sheet, glossary, and score tracker

Ideal for students enrolled in AP Biology, for summer preparation, and for review before unit tests and the AP Exam.
