3. Facilitated diffusion through a carrier protein

4. Osmosis through an aquaporin

5. Primary active transport (ATP-powered pump, such as the Na+/K+ pump)

6. Symport (cotransporter): both substances move in the same direction

7. Antiport (exchanger): the two substances move in opposite directions

8. Endocytosis

9. Exocytosis

10. Panels 1–4 (passive transport down a gradient)

11. Panel 5 uses ATP directly. Panels 6 and 7 are secondary active transport: one substance moves down its gradient and powers the uphill movement of the other. Panels 8 and 9 also need energy.
