Part 3 · Extra Practice

Who Am I? Vocabulary Challenge

Precise vocabulary earns points on the AP Biology Exam. Each clue below describes an organelle, structure, molecule, or process from Unit 2. Write the term that best fits each clue. Answers are in Part 4.

How to Use This Section

Work one section at a time. Mark any clue you had to guess, then reread that part of the lessons before checking the answers. Return to the marked clues a few days later.

Section A · Organelles and Cell Types

1. I am the double-membrane structure that stores a eukaryotic cell’s DNA. Who am I?

2. I am the dense region inside the nucleus where ribosomal subunits are assembled. What am I?

3. I am the openings in the nuclear envelope that let mRNA leave and proteins enter. What am I?

4. I am built of rRNA and protein, have no membrane, and am found in every living cell. What am I?

5. I am a network of membrane sacs studded with ribosomes, where proteins for secretion are made. What am I?

6. I am a network of membrane tubules without ribosomes that makes lipids and detoxifies drugs. What am I?

7. I am a stack of flattened sacs with a cis face and a trans face that modifies, sorts, and ships proteins. What am I?

8. I am a small membrane sac that carries materials between parts of the endomembrane system. What am I?

9. I am a membrane sac of hydrolytic enzymes that work best at about pH 5. What am I?

11. I am programmed cell death, which lysosomes can help carry out. What am I?

12. I am the organelle that produces most of a eukaryotic cell’s ATP through cellular respiration. What am I?

13. I am the folds of the inner mitochondrial membrane that increase its surface area. What am I?

14. I am the fluid inside the inner mitochondrial membrane. What am I?

15. I am the organelle that carries out photosynthesis in plants and algae. What am I?

16. I am the stacked membrane sacs inside a chloroplast where the light reactions occur. What am I?

17. I am the fluid around the thylakoids where the Calvin cycle occurs. What am I?

18. I am the large, water-filled sac that creates turgor pressure in plant cells. What am I?

19. I am the membrane surrounding the central vacuole. What am I?

20. I am a single-membrane organelle that uses catalase to break down hydrogen peroxide. What am I?

21. I am the network of protein fibers that gives a cell its shape and moves vesicles and chromosomes. What am I?

22. I am the hollow tubes of tubulin that form cilia, flagella, and the spindle. What am I?

23. I am the thin actin fibers involved in muscle contraction and changes in cell shape. What am I?

24. I am a pair of structures made of microtubule triplets, found in the centrosome of animal cells. What am I?

25. I am the rigid layer outside the plasma membrane of plant cells, made of cellulose. What am I?

26. I am the channels through plant cell walls that connect the cytoplasm of neighboring cells. What am I?

27. I am the region of a prokaryotic cell that contains its DNA but has no membrane around it. What am I?

28. I am a small, circular piece of extra DNA often found in bacteria. What am I?

29. I am the type of cell that has a nucleus and membrane-bound organelles. What am I?

30. I am the group of organelles—nuclear envelope, ER, Golgi, lysosomes, vacuoles, vesicles, and plasma membrane—that work together to make and ship proteins and lipids. What am I?

45. I am the region of the membrane that blocks ions and large polar molecules. What am I?

Section C · Transport, Tonicity, and Water Potential

56. I am a difference in the concentration of a substance between two regions. What am I?

57. I am the net movement of particles from higher to lower concentration. What am I?

58. I am the state in which molecules still move but there is no net change in concentration. What am I?

59. I am movement across a membrane down a gradient with no energy input from the cell. What am I?

60. I am passive movement through a channel or carrier protein. What am I?

61. I am a protein that binds a substance and changes shape to move it across the membrane. What am I?

62. I am a channel that opens or closes in response to a stimulus such as voltage or a signal molecule. What am I?

63. I am the condition in which all carrier proteins are busy, so the rate of transport stops increasing. What am I?

64. I am movement against a concentration gradient that requires energy. What am I?

65. I am the pump that uses ATP to move 3 Na+ out of and 2 K+ into an animal cell. What am I?

66. I am the addition of a phosphate group from ATP that changes the shape of a pump. What am I?

67. I am the voltage across a membrane caused by an unequal distribution of ions. What am I?

68. I am the combined effect of an ion’s concentration gradient and the charge difference across a membrane. What am I?

69. I am transport in which one substance moving down its gradient powers another substance moving up its gradient. What am I?

70. I am an active transporter that pumps H+ out of plant cells. What am I?

71. I am the diffusion of water across a selectively permeable membrane. What am I?

72. I am a solution with a higher solute concentration than the cell. What am I?

73. I am a solution with a lower solute concentration than the cell. What am I?

74. I am a solution with the same solute concentration as the cell. What am I?

75. I am the shrinking of a plant cell’s cytoplasm away from its wall in a hypertonic solution. What am I?

76. I am the firm condition of a plant cell in a hypotonic solution. What am I?

77. I am the measure of water’s tendency to move; water moves from high to low values of me. What am I?

78. I am the component of water potential caused by dissolved particles; I equal −iCRT. What am I?

79. I am the organelle that pumps excess water out of freshwater protists. What am I?

80. I am the control of water balance and solute concentration in an organism. What am I?

Section D · Bulk Transport, Cell Size, and Compartments

95. I am the group of photosynthetic bacteria most closely related to chloroplasts. What am I?
